Where Green Pastures is

Green Pastures is a populated place in Union County that place directories list within the City of Marysville, about a mile and a half from the center of town. Listing sites show several sections, including a Retreat at Green Pastures, which tells us the neighborhood grew in phases.

We have no official boundary map for it, so we describe it by name and not by streets. Give dispatch your street and the nearest cross street when you call.

Built in sections, so equipment varies

One local agent guide describes Green Pastures homes as built between 1994 and 2004, with newer construction in later sections. A house from the late 1990s may be on its second water heater and its original supply lines, while a newer one is on its first. That difference matters more to a leak than the neighborhood does.

We do not assume older homes leak more, and we do not claim any plumbing material without seeing it. The useful questions are how old the water heater is, whether the hoses are rubber or braided and where the shutoffs are.

Checks that prevent the common leaks

  • Look at the water heater tank and floor for rust, drips or a damp drain pan.
  • Replace aging rubber washing machine hoses.
  • Check under kitchen and bathroom sinks for soft cabinet bases.
  • Find the main shutoff and make sure it turns.
  • Check the attic and ceilings after storms for new stains.

Our water heater leak page and appliance leak page describe what happens after one of these lets go.

When the leak is upstairs

Two-story homes in this part of Marysville often have bathrooms above kitchens and living rooms. A tub overflow or a failed fitting upstairs can soak a ceiling and the wall cavity below. Stay clear of a bulging ceiling and keep lights on that ceiling switched off until it has been checked. See ceiling water damage.

What to do while you wait

Shut it off

Close the fixture valve or the main when you can reach it dry.

Stay out of danger

Do not touch outlets or breakers while standing in water.

Photograph first

Record the damage and the source before moving anything.

Lift and protect

Move furniture, rugs and small items to dry spots.

Flood and storm context

Insurance and records

Ask your insurer what your policy says about the cause of the water. Keep photos, receipts and any moisture readings, since they describe what was found and what was done. They do not guarantee that a claim is approved, and coverage terms vary.
